# /think:grill

1. Restate the idea in two sentences and ask Keshav to confirm.
2. Ask one question at a time, the sharpest first. Target: hidden assumptions, missing numbers,
   failure modes, cheaper alternatives, what happens at 10x, who operates it.
3. When a factual claim decides the question, check it (docs or `think:docs-reader`) before you
   argue from it.
4. Stop after the idea breaks, or after about eight questions.
5. Summarize: **held up**, **broke**, **unknown**, **next step**.
